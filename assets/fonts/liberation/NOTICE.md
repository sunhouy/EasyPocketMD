Liberation Serif / Sans provide metric-compatible fallbacks for Times New Roman
and Arial in local PDF exports. Font files are lazy loaded only for the selected
family; they are also packaged in native builds. Fonts are not renamed or sold
separately. HTML and server exports prefer the requested installed font.
